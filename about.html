Behind Aura Service

/ Our Story

We started Aura Service Info on 27 April 2024 with one belief: ideas deserve more than just possibilities.

Story & Vision
Aura Service Info is a technology and business-growth company from Thiruvananthapuram and a part of KBG Business Solutions Pvt. Ltd. We help startups, businesses and enterprises build, digitalize and accelerate, bringing websites, apps, custom software, branding, digital marketing and startup support under one roof. Our vision is to be the partner a business can grow with, from the first idea to large-scale technology.
