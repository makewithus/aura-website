/ FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can apply for the Startup Initiative?

Early-stage startups and first-time founders with a clear idea or a newly registered business can apply. Final eligibility and terms are shared during review.

What is included in a startup package?

Packages can cover company registration and GST support, legal guidance, branding, a business website, launch marketing and mentorship. We tailor the mix to your stage.

Is this a free programme?

No. It is an affordable, startup-friendly package with fair pricing and flexible payment terms, delivered by the same specialists who serve our business clients.

How long does the process take?

It depends on the services you need. After review we share a clear plan and timeline before any work starts.

Do you support startups after launch?

Yes. We continue with marketing, website updates, apps and IT support, and can scale up to enterprise projects with our partner Make With Us.

How do we get started?

Fill in the enquiry form or message us on WhatsApp at +91 7591 929 595. We will schedule a free initial consultation.

What if my project is too large for a startup package?

Larger web apps, enterprise software and custom platforms are handled through Enterprise Development with Make With Us, our strategic development partner.

Can established businesses work with you too?

Yes. Beyond the Startup Initiative, we work with growing businesses and enterprises across all of our services.
